Our Story

A small room with a big fire

Savora began in 2012 as a twelve seat counter around a secondhand oven. Today the room seats eighty, but the ritual is the same: light the oak at dawn, bake bread at noon, welcome guests at five.

We buy from nine nearby farms and bake everything in house. Nothing rushed, nothing wasted.

14Years of fire
9Partner farms
80Seats nightly
The oven never sleeps

Kept at a steady glow from dawn till close, our oak oven bakes, roasts, and chars everything you taste.
